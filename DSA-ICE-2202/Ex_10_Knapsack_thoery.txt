### Theory

The **0/1 Knapsack Problem** is an optimization problem where we have a number of items, and each item has a **profit** and a **weight**. We have a knapsack with a fixed capacity, and our goal is to select items so that the **total profit is maximum** without exceeding the capacity.

In **0/1 Knapsack**, each item can either be **selected (1)** or **not selected (0)**. An item cannot be selected more than once.

In this program, the **Dynamic Programming** approach is used. A table `dp[i][j]` is used to store the maximum profit obtained using the first `i` items with capacity `j`.

### Algorithm

1. **Start** the program.
2. Initialize the number of items `n`, knapsack capacity `C`, profits `P[]`, and weights `W[]`.
3. Create a DP table `dp[n+1][C+1]` and initialize it with `0`.
4. Set `i = 1` and consider each item one by one.
5. For each capacity `j` from `0` to `C`:

   * If the weight of the current item is less than or equal to `j`, choose the maximum between:

     * Not taking the item.
     * Taking the item and adding its profit.
   * Otherwise, do not take the item.
6. Store the maximum profit in `dp[i][j]`.
7. Repeat the process for all items.
8. The value `dp[n][C]` gives the maximum possible profit.
9. Display the maximum profit.
10. **Stop** the program.
