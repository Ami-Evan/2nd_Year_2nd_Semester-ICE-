### Objective

1. To compare the t-distribution with the standard normal distribution.
2. To observe the difference between the two distributions for a small degree of freedom.
3. To understand that the t-distribution has heavier tails than the normal distribution for small sample sizes.
4. To visualize both distributions using R.

### Theory

The **t-distribution** is a continuous probability distribution that is commonly used for statistical analysis when the sample size is small and the population standard deviation is unknown.

The shape of the t-distribution depends on the **degrees of freedom (df)**. For a small value of df, the t-distribution has **heavier tails** and a slightly lower peak than the standard normal distribution. This represents greater uncertainty caused by a small sample size.

As the degrees of freedom increase, the t-distribution becomes closer to the standard normal distribution.

In this experiment, the standard normal distribution and t-distribution with **df = 5** are plotted and compared using R.

### Pseudocode

```text

Set degrees of freedom, df = 5

Generate x values from -4 to 4
```
