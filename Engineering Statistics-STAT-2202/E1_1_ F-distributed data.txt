## Experiment: Simulate F-Distributed Data and Study Its Relationship with Chi-Square Distributions

### Theory

The **F-distribution** can be constructed using two independent chi-square random variables. If \(X_1\) and \(X_2\) follow chi-square distributions with \(df_1\) and \(df_2\) degrees of freedom, then:

$$
F = \frac{X_1/df_1}{X_2/df_2}
$$

In this experiment, two chi-square samples are generated with **df₁ = 5** and **df₂ = 10**. Their ratio is used to generate F-distributed data. A histogram of the simulated F-data is plotted, and the theoretical F-density curve is added for comparison.

### Objectives

1. To understand the F-distribution.
2. To generate chi-square distributed random samples.
3. To construct F-distributed data from two chi-square distributions.
4. To compare the simulated F-distribution with its theoretical density.
5. To compare the simulated mean with the theoretical mean.

### Pseudocode

```text
START

1. Set random seed.

2. Set:
   n = 1000
   df1 = 5
   df2 = 10

3. Generate the first chi-square sample
   with df1 degrees of freedom.

4. Generate the second chi-square sample
   with df2 degrees of freedom.

5. Calculate F-data using:
   F = (chi1 / df1) / (chi2 / df2)

6. Calculate the mean of simulated F-data.

7. Calculate the theoretical mean of F-distribution.

8. Display both mean values.

9. Draw a histogram of F-data.

10. Add the theoretical F-density curve
    to the histogram.

11. Save the graph as a PDF file.

END
```
