

### Objective

* To generate random data from a **Chi-square distribution**.
* To calculate the **sample mean and sample variance**.
* To compare the sample values with their **theoretical values**.
* To plot a histogram and compare it with the **theoretical Chi-square density curve**.

### Theory

The **Chi-square distribution** is a continuous probability distribution used in statistical analysis. It is defined by its **degrees of freedom (df)**.

For a Chi-square distribution with `df` degrees of freedom:

* **Theoretical Mean = df**
* **Theoretical Variance = 2 × df**

In this experiment, `df = 5` and 1000 random observations are generated. The sample mean and variance are calculated and compared with the theoretical mean and variance. A histogram is plotted to observe the distribution, and the theoretical density curve is added for comparison.
